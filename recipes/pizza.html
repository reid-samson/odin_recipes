Pizza

Description

This is a pizza recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Top with the grated Parmesan cheese.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
