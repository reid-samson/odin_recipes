Breadsticks

Description

This is a breadsticks recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, combine warm water, yeast, and sugar. Let it sit for 5 minutes until frothy.
  2. In a large bowl, mix flour and salt. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil. Stir until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Punch down the dough and divide it into 12 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a breadstick shape and place them on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  6. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown. Serve warm with marinara sauce or garlic butter.
